Discover Nature

Located in the Amminikkadan Hills of Malappuram district, Kodikuthimala stands tall at an altitude of 522 meters above sea level. Often referred to as the "Ooty of Malappuram", this destination offers a cool climate and rolling hills that mimic the famous hill station.

Whether you are a trekking enthusiast or someone looking to escape the city heat, the panoramic 360-degree views of the Western Ghats from the summit will leave you mesmerized.

The Story Behind the Name

The name Kodikuthimala translates literally to "Flag Hoisted Hill" in Malayalam.


During the British colonial era in India, the British Survey Team identified this peak as a strategic point for their geographical surveys. To mark this significance, they hoisted a flag at the summit. The locals began referring to the hill as the place where the flag (Kodi) was stuck (Kuthi), giving rise to its current name.

Why Visit?

🗼

The Watch Tower

Climb the 3-story watchtower at the summit for a stunning 360-degree view of Perinthalmanna town and the distant horizons.

🥾

Trekking Trail

A moderate 1.5 km trek from the base to the top. The path is scenic, especially during the monsoon season when springs come alive.

🌫️

Misty Climate

Experience the chill breeze and fog that covers the hilltops in the early mornings and evenings, earning its nickname.

🌄

Valley View Point

Locally known as Suicide Point, this steep cliff offers a thrilling vertical drop view and is perfect for photography.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reach Kodikuthimala? +

The nearest town is Perinthalmanna. You can drive up to the parking area near the ticket counter. From there, it is a short trek to the top. Nearest Railway station is Angadipuram (15km).

Is it suitable for families? +

Yes, it is a family-friendly destination. However, the trek might be difficult for the elderly or those with mobility issues.

Are food facilities available at the top? +

No, there are no shops at the peak. It is highly recommended to carry water and light snacks, but please do not litter.
